# Verification Choreography

## Bridge (items 1–4)

The bridge runs the live half, per their standing capability:

- after your fix deploys, they re-run the streaming Play session that
  triggered the incident, at the segment budget you green-light, and
  file RSS/stability observations back into the OOM request dir —
  closing their `l1w` if it holds;
- they own the worker restart procedure and the deploy window
  (their `72o` lease caveat); coordinate timing, don't restart under
  their live sessions.

## reference-workload (item 5)

Their round-2 corpus request
(`../reference-workload/.agents/requests/phase4-real-capture-corpus-fast-follow/`)
consumes your sample capture evidence as the engine-side proof beneath
their packaging run. If a capture-engine defect surfaces during their
corpus production, it files back here — the engine is yours, the corpus
is theirs.

## Phases-Track Verification

On your resolution we will:

1. re-run the RSS regression guard from a clean checkout;
2. verify the record/replay gate citation is a real green run at the
   fix commit;
3. for item 5, re-verify one sampled extraction range against
   `detguest-host` independently.

## Handback Shape

Append `04-resolution.md` here (bead id, fix commits, profile evidence
path, guard location + bound derivation, `9bx` answer, capture-sample
paths or the waiting note); we respond with `05-verification.md`.
Also write the resolution file into
`requests/run-with-frame-capture-memory-leak-oom/` — that dir is the
incident's public record and must not dangle.
